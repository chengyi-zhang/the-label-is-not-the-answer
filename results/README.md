# Aggregate results

Only aggregate, non-case-level results are released here. The directories correspond to the paper and supplementary analyses:

- `anchor_intervention/`: Qwen2.5-VL-3B completed-example intervention.
- `canonical/`: primary, holdout, and model-scale canonical-order comparisons.
- `cyclic/`: five-way reassignment accuracy and output-agreement summaries.
- `factorial/`: the 2 x 2 completed-example cue by option-prefix control.
- `cross_family/`: InternVL3-9B completed-example check and audit summary.

Raw participant records, case IDs selected for qualitative inspection, model rationales, images, and raw generations are intentionally excluded.
